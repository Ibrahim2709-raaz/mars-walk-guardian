# Mars Walk Guardian

Mars Walk Guardian is a NASA Space Apps MVP concept for safety-first extravehicular activity (EVA) route planning in the boxwork terrain of the Mount Sharp region of Gale Crater, Mars.

The concept centers on a simulated habitat, **Gale Base**, near the provisional coordinate **4.842° S, 137.419° E** (latitude `-4.842`, longitude `137.419`). It combines NASA-derived datasets to help plan safer, scientifically useful traverses:

- HiRISE or CTX optical imagery for surface context
- MOLA elevation and derived slope information for terrain risk
- CRISM mineral context for identifying potential science targets
- Weighted route planning that balances safety, traversal cost, and science value
- Emergency rerouting based on simulated oxygen, battery, temperature, and daylight conditions

## Safety and data disclaimer

Mars Walk Guardian is an educational MVP and decision-support concept, not operational flight software. **The Gale Base habitat coordinate is simulated and does not represent NASA-approved mission infrastructure. All suit telemetry—including oxygen, battery, temperature, and daylight status—is simulated.** Routes and risk estimates must not be interpreted as NASA-approved EVA plans.

NASA-derived imagery and planetary datasets remain subject to their respective source metadata, attribution guidance, and usage terms.
